## Request for district-to-district travel and migration tabulations from the Census of Population and Housing

We are a final-year undergraduate group in the Department of Computer Science and
Engineering, University of Moratuwa, working on a research project that forecasts
district-level dengue incidence in Sri Lanka. The project is supervised by
**[ supervisor's name and title ]** and is unfunded academic work.

We already use the Department's published district population data — the Census
2012 district reports and the mid-year population estimates by district — and
they are recorded with their sources in our project documentation.

### Why we are asking

Our model treats the 25 districts as a network. At present two districts are
connected if they share a border. We have tested that assumption and found it
contributes nothing measurable to forecast accuracy, which is unsurprising:
dengue is carried between districts by **people**, and *Aedes* mosquitoes
themselves travel only a few hundred metres. A district-to-district travel matrix
would replace a geographic assumption with a measured one.

### Data requested

All aggregate. **No individual-level or household-level data is requested.**

| Variable | Granularity | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Place of work or study by district of residence — an origin–destination matrix | district × district | Census 2012 (and 2024, if tabulated) |
| Lifetime or recent internal migration by district of birth or previous residence and district of enumeration | district × district | Census 2012 |
| Any published estimate of daily commuting volume between districts | district × district | any year |

A single 25 × 25 matrix would be sufficient. We understand it would be static
rather than a time series, and that is acceptable for our purpose — the
structure of travel between districts is what the model needs, not its
week-to-week variation.

**If these tabulations are already published**, a pointer to the correct report
or table number would be just as helpful as a data file, and we would be glad to
extract the figures ourselves.

### Purpose and handling

- Purpose: research only, to construct a movement-based spatial structure for a
  dengue forecasting model in place of geographic adjacency.
- Aggregate use only; no redistribution of any file provided.
- Findings published openly, with the Department of Census and Statistics
  credited.
- If there is a standard application form or fee schedule for custom
  tabulations, we would be grateful to be pointed to it.
