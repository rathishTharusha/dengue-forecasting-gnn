\begin{abstract}
Spatio-temporal graph neural networks (ST-GNNs) and SEIR-informed neural models
are both proposed for district-level dengue forecasting, but they are rarely
compared under one leak-free protocol. We study whether replacing the LSTM of an
SEIR-informed forecaster with a graph encoder helps, on Sri Lanka's weekly record
(25 districts, three-week horizon). Before modelling, we audit the benchmark
array used by prior work: its rows are out of date order, every training split
contains 2023 data from after the test periods, its case and weather columns are
on different timelines, its ``lag 12'' rainfall channel holds rain from twelve
weeks \emph{ahead}, and its largest spike is a spreadsheet error. We rebuild a
559-week dataset from hash-verified original sources, and one self-contained
notebook reproduces every result from them. On one harness, six encoders (five
published ST-GNNs and the SEIR-LSTM's LSTM) are each paired with a direct head, a
pure SEIR decoder and a gated SEIR head. Architecture matters little: the working
encoders span 0.20 RMSE and the graph adds 0.06. The pure SEIR decoder is worse
than persistence on every encoder, and 7.3--7.4 RMSE worse than the direct head
on each working one, for structural reasons: 14--16\% of targets lie below its
zero-transmission floor and its force of infection is only $r^2\approx0.25$
predictable. A gated SEIR head, which lets the simulator supply only the
departure from persistence, brings all six encoders below persistence and repairs
the three that fail as published by 5.3--17.0 validation RMSE on 9/9 paired runs.
A pre-registered control shows that this repair is not physics: the same head
without the simulator accounts for 96--112\% of it, and adding the simulator is
significantly worse on one failing encoder, not better on the other two, and
0.75--0.86 worse on the working ones. A negative-binomial likelihood is the
largest controlled gain ($-0.76$, 9/9). The graph beats the LSTM inside the gated
SEIR head on three origins, and that advantage is not robust on nine disjoint
origins. More than twenty further levers are not adopted, and the working models
make the same errors (residual correlation 0.91--0.97). On this target the useful
prior is persistence, not transmission dynamics.
\end{abstract}
