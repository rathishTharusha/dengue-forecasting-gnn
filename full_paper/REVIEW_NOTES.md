# Full paper — notes for co-author review

Draft of 2026-09-24: `full_paper.pdf` (8 pages including references), source in
`overleaf/` (upload `overleaf.zip` to Overleaf).

## What changed from the Phase-2 paper

The Phase-2 short paper (*Know When the Epidemic Comes*, kept unchanged in `paper/`)
was built on the benchmark array. This paper is rebuilt on the corrected dataset and
reports the SEIR-GNN study the project set out to do.

- **New data section.** The benchmark array has rows out of date order, 2023 data in
  every training split, and "lagged" climate channels that hold values 12–17 weeks in
  the future. The week-395 spike is a spreadsheet error. It is **not** an
  administrative backlog, which is what the Phase-2 paper said.
- **New results.**
  - Six encoders × SEIR heads on one harness.
  - Every lever tried, in one paired table.
  - Both nine-origin confirmations.
  - A control showing the gated SEIR head's repair of STGAT, A3TGCN and DCRNN is
    the persistence anchor, not the physics.
- **Dropped.** The Phase-2 increment table (per-horizon heads, attention, Huber,
  Gaussian head) and the renewal/early-warning sections. These were measured on the
  flawed array. The renewal and Moran's I findings are cited only as array-based
  results.

## What reviewers should check

- **Numbers in the prose match the tables.** Tables 2–5 and Figures 1–3 are
  generated by `scripts/build_paper_assets.py` from `seirgnn2/results/*.json`.
  Numbers in the prose were copied by hand and are the likeliest place for an error.
- **Every claim has its caveat.** Three origins cannot support claims at the
  origin unit. The nine-origin test spans were used twice. The two confirmations
  named different endpoints.
- **Citations.** Five were added: Liu 2025, Cao 2023, Shao 2022, Kim 2022 and
  Salinas 2020. Check them against the PDFs in `literature/pdfs/`.

```bash
  sh -c "pdflatex main && bibtex main && pdflatex main && pdflatex main"
```

Or upload `overleaf.zip` to Overleaf. (`paper/build.sh` builds the Phase-2 paper,
not this one.)

uses EXP-032–EXP-048.
