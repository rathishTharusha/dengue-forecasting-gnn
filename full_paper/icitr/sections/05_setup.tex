\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\textbf{Dataset.} All development results use the corrected 559-week series
(Section~\ref{sec:data}); the 2024--2026 weeks are used only for the prospective test.
Every model, baseline and control is scored on exactly the same validation and test
windows and trained on the same training windows, except that arms with climate lags
skip the first training weeks, which have no climate history that far back. Windows
that touch a missing week are dropped for all of them.

\textbf{Baselines.} Persistence $\hat y_{t+h}=y_t$ and seasonal naive
$\hat y_{t+h}=y_{t+h-52}$; the mandatory sequence and graph baselines, the two-layer
LSTM of SEIR-LSTM~\cite{liu2025seirlstm} and a GCN~\cite{kipf2017gcn} against the same
network without message passing; the five ST-GNNs of the Sri Lankan
benchmark~\cite{weng2024graph} (STGAT, A3TGCN, ASTGCN, AAGCN, DCRNN) with their
published direct heads; SEIR-informed heads (pure SEIR decoder, SEIR-LSTM, SEIR-GNN);
and non-neural learners: gradient-boosted trees~\cite{chen2016xgboost} with and
without climate, a negative-binomial GLM, $k$-nearest-neighbour analogues, an AR(3)
ridge regression and an STID-style MLP~\cite{shao2022stid}.

\textbf{Protocol.} Rolling origins at 0.55, 0.70 and 0.85 of the series, with three
seeds each; the 30 windows before each origin are validation and the next 15\% are
test, with no shuffling across time. Selection uses validation RMSE only, and test
numbers never choose a configuration. The protocol, and for each experiment its arms,
control and decision rule, were committed before it ran. Differences are paired on
matched (origin, seed) units and tested with an exact sign-flip permutation test,
Benjamini--Hochberg adjusted. The prospective test trains once on all development
weeks, freezes every setting, and scores 85 forecast starts from 2024-W11, with no
target week shared between partitions; it uses a block bootstrap over forecast starts
(block 8, 10{,}000 replicates) with Holm correction over two pre-registered comparisons.
RMSE in weekly cases, pooled over windows, districts and horizons, is the primary
metric; MAE, SMAPE and MAPE are secondary.
