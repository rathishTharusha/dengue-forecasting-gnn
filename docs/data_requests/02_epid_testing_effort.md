## Request for aggregate testing and notification metadata for an undergraduate dengue forecasting study

We are a final-year undergraduate group in the Department of Computer Science and
Engineering, University of Moratuwa, working on a research project that forecasts
district-level dengue incidence in Sri Lanka. The project is supervised by
**[ supervisor's name and title ]** and is unfunded academic work.

The project already uses the Unit's published Weekly Epidemiological Reports. We
have digitised Table 1, row A, for every district from 2013 to 2024 and verified
each value against the published PDF — all 553 reports and 14,341 district values
match the originals exactly. We are grateful for how completely the Unit
publishes this series; it is the backbone of the study.

The request below is for the context that the weekly counts do not carry.

### Why we are asking

A notification count mixes two different things: how much disease there is, and
how much testing and reporting is happening. Our model currently has to represent
that mixture with a single fixed constant. This is visible in the data — during
2020 national notifications fall by roughly 90%, and we cannot presently
distinguish how much of that fall was a real drop in transmission from how much
was reduced care-seeking and reduced testing.

### Data requested

All aggregate. **No individual-level or patient-identifiable data is requested.**

| Variable | Granularity | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Number of specimens tested for dengue | district × month (national × month acceptable) | 2013 to present |
| Confirmations by test type (NS1 antigen / IgM / PCR) | district × month | 2013 to present |
| Dengue hospital admissions, if held separately from notifications | district × month | 2013 to present |
| Notified cases by age group (standard bands) | district × month, or national × month | 2013 to present |
| Dengue haemorrhagic fever / severe dengue counts | district × month | 2013 to present |
| Approximate share of notifications originating from private-sector institutions | national × year | 2013 to present |

The age breakdown is requested for a specific reason: a shift in the age
distribution of cases is an early signature of a change in circulating serotype,
and it may be observable in notification records before it is confirmed
virologically.

**If the full series is not readily available**, national totals by month, or a
shorter period from 2016 onward, would still be useful.

### Purpose and handling

- Purpose: research only, to separate reporting effort from disease incidence in
  a forecasting model.
- Aggregate use only; no redistribution of any file provided.
- Findings published openly with the Epidemiology Unit credited.
- We would be glad to share our digitised and verified 2013–2024 district case
  table, the verification results against every published report, and our code,
  with the Unit at any point — including the two data-quality observations we
  recorded while verifying: one report whose weekly row appears to contain a
  spreadsheet formula error, and sixteen reports where the printed national
  cumulative total does not equal the sum of the printed districts. We can supply
  the exact report references for these.
