# Full paper — notes for co-author review

Draft of 2026-09-25: `full_paper.pdf` (8 pages including references), source in
`overleaf/` (upload `overleaf.zip` to Overleaf).

## What changed from the Phase-2 paper

The Phase-2 short paper (*Know When the Epidemic Comes*, kept unchanged in `paper/`)
was built on the benchmark array. This paper is rebuilt on the corrected dataset and
reports the SEIR-GNN study the project set out to do.

- **New data section.** The benchmark array has rows out of date order, 2023 data in
  every training split, and "lagged" climate channels that hold values 12–17 weeks in
  the future. The week-395 spike is a spreadsheet error. It is **not** an
  administrative backlog, which is what the Phase-2 paper said.
- **New results.**
  - Six encoders × SEIR heads on one harness.
  - Every lever tried, in one paired table.
  - Both nine-origin confirmations.
  - A control showing the gated SEIR head's repair of STGAT, A3TGCN and DCRNN is
    the persistence anchor, not the physics.
- **Dropped.** The Phase-2 increment table (per-horizon heads, attention, Huber,
  Gaussian head) and the renewal/early-warning sections. These were measured on the
  flawed array. The renewal and Moran's I findings are cited only as array-based
  results.

## Revised 2026-09-25

- Corrected facts: on the corrected data lag-1 r² is **0.89** and the best causal climate
  covariate 0.03 (0.85 / 0.02 were benchmark-array numbers); the pure SEIR decoder is worse
  than *persistence* on every encoder, not worse than the direct head on every encoder;
  "36.02" is the mean over origins, like every model's number.
- Added the EXP-049 finding to the introduction: the first pipeline's "win" compared one
  origin (26.1) with persistence averaged over three (36.0); at that origin persistence
  scored 22.4.
- The levers table (Table 3) now has 28 rows and is the same list the notebook uses, so
  the two report identical p-values.
- The validation/test section is now quantified across the run's families of configurations.

## Revised again 2026-09-25: every number from one notebook run (EXP-050)

- The whole study was re-run in **one Kaggle notebook** (`kaggle/dengue_physics_gnn.ipynb`)
  that reads only original source files (hash-verified), rebuilds the data, defines the five
  published architectures and the LSTM in plain PyTorch, and trains all 810 runs. The paper's
  tables, figures and prose numbers now come from that run (`outputs/kaggle_run/`), not from
  results accumulated across EXP-032–048.
- Numbers that moved as a result (all in the text now): NB likelihood −0.76, 9/9 (was −0.41);
  best model B 15.51 validation / 37.54 test; residual correlation 0.91–0.97; the simulator's
  cost on the working encoders 0.75–0.86; the rescue credit test is unchanged (none credited
  to physics). The qualitative conclusions did not change.
- New: SEIR-GNN vs SEIR-LSTM on nine origins now reports both encoders on both metrics
  (ASTGCN −0.62 validation / +0.07 test; AAGCN +0.18 / −0.30). Neither is robust.
- Removed: the old `reproduce_full_paper.ipynb`, which re-analysed committed rows rather
  than training, and the legacy `outputs/` files from the S5 pipeline.

## What reviewers should check

- **Numbers in the prose match the tables.** Tables 2–5 and Figures 1–3 are generated by
  `scripts/build_paper_assets.py` from `outputs/kaggle_run/`. Numbers in the prose were
  copied by hand from the same files and the notebook's printed output
  (`outputs/kaggle_run/notebook_output.txt`); they are the likeliest place for an error.
- **Every claim has its caveat.** Three origins cannot support claims at the origin unit.
  Validation and test disagree in most families.
- **Two results are not from the notebook.** §3.1 (the reproduction of Weng et al.) runs
  the authors' own code (`reproduction/`), and the −0.044 spatial-penalty result is cited
  from the Phase-2 study on the benchmark array.
- **One input has no public URL.** The parsed weekly-report table was received from the
  benchmark authors; the notebook verifies it against its recorded hash, and
  `docs/DATA_PROVENANCE.md` records its check against the 553 official PDFs.
- **Citations.** Five were added: Liu 2025, Cao 2023, Shao 2022, Kim 2022 and
  Salinas 2020. Check them against the PDFs in `literature/pdfs/`.
