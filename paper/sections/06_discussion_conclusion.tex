\section{Discussion and Conclusion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\cB{\textbf{When does a mechanistic prior pay?} Physics-informed networks succeed
where data are scarce relative to the dynamics: MP-PINN~\cite{nguyen2024mppinn}
trains on 35 days and forecasts 97, a regime in which a data-driven model has
almost nothing to learn from. Ours is the opposite. The decisive quantity is not
sample size but how much of the target a naive baseline already explains. Here the
previous week explains $r^2=0.85$; the mechanistic decomposition divides that out
and leaves $R_t$, which is 26\% predictable. A prior helps when it supplies
structure the data cannot, and hurts when it routes a well-determined quantity
through a poorly-determined one. That ratio, not the sample size, is the criterion
we would apply before attempting this again. A physics term that fails at one
weighting is a tuning anecdote; one that cannot succeed at any weighting, because
the quantity it depends on carries a $1.90\times$ error, is a finding.}

\cC{\textbf{Implications for benchmarking.} Three inexpensive practices would have
changed the published conclusions on this dataset: report a naive baseline, pool
squared error rather than averaging per window, and inspect the test split for
administrative artifacts before reporting fold-level results. The last is the least
standard and here the largest: it moves the floor by 15 RMSE.}

\cD{\textbf{Limitations.} Conclusions concern one country, one disease and one
processed array; the artifact analysis is dataset-specific, though the practice it
motivates is not. The adaptive-graph comparison rests on $n=9$ and is unsettled, we
do not evaluate a GAN, and the detection result uses a single-week threshold; the
median episode lasts one week, so sustained exceedance is the better target and our
next step.}

\cE{\textbf{Conclusion.} Under a leak-free protocol with artifact-aware dual
scoring, no reproduced architecture and no evidence-led increment separates from a
persistence floor of 29.52 at $p<0.05$, and the mechanistic prior the task invites
cannot close that gap: the ceiling here is informational, not architectural. The
same quantity that fails at forecasting is the largest single contributor to
ranking outbreaks, which is where mechanistic structure belongs on such data. Code
and a notebook regenerating every number are in the repository.}
